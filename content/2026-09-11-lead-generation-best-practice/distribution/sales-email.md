# Sales email (1:1 template)

Derived from `source.md`. For a rep to personalize and send individually to one prospect — not a broadcast/blast template. Per the snapshot's Channels section (Targeted email): "Write like a relevant personal note, not a mass lead-sales campaign." Bracketed fields are for the rep to fill in.

**Compliance placement rule, carried through from the snapshot:** email must identify Sanguine, state why the recipient is being contacted, and provide an unsubscribe method where required. Reps: don't drop the sign-off block below even though this reads as a personal note.

---

**Subject:** quick thought on [Firm Name]'s lead-gen setup

[First Name],

Following up because [reason for reaching out — e.g., "we spoke briefly at [event]" / "you mentioned you're reviewing lead providers" / "I noticed [Firm Name] is expanding into [market/case type]"].

Most firms vet a lead vendor with the same handful of questions: is the source generating its own leads, is it exclusive, what's the consent process. Good questions. The problem is a vendor comfortable being vetted gives a comfortable answer, and that answer can't tell you whether the source will actually hold up against your intake team and your case mix.

That's the gap we built our process around. We treat vetting as stage one of four (vet, test, deliver, manage), test a source's real-world performance before it goes to any firm, and stay involved after a firm signs to catch the things a questionnaire never can, like whether delivery still holds 90 days in.

Worth ten minutes to compare notes on how [Firm Name] is currently vetting and running third-party lead generation?

**CTA:** Request a Lead-Generation Review, free and no obligation: [REVIEW]

(Or, if a lower-commitment first step fits better: happy to just send over the full write-up first — [ARTICLE])

[Rep name]
[Rep title, Sanguine Legal Solutions]
