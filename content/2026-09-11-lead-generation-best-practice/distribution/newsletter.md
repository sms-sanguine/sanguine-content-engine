**Subject line / section header:** A lead source is only as strong as what happens after launch

A clean answer to a vendor's vetting questionnaire proves one thing: the vendor is good at answering questions. It doesn't prove the source will work for your firm, your case types, your intake team.

That gap is exactly where lead-generation budget tends to disappear. It's also why Sanguine Legal Solutions treats vetting as the first of four stages, not the whole process: vet, test, deliver, manage. A source gets tested against real performance before it's introduced more broadly, and we stay involved after a firm signs, watching for the things a questionnaire can't reach, like whether volume and quality still hold 90 days in, not week one.

If your firm is currently running third-party lead generation, or evaluating a new provider, this is worth ten minutes.
